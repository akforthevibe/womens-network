11 Questions

Before you ask.

Something else? Write to hello@decodingdraupadi.com.

Is this a women's networking group?

No. It's a professional network built around introductions. You don't join and then hope to bump into the right person. You tell us what you need, and we find her.

Who can apply?

Established women in marketing, media and creative work in Mumbai: real experience behind you, a next move in front of you, and something you can offer another woman.

Is membership paid?

Yes. Founding membership is ₹12,000 a year. You only pay once you've been accepted.

What does membership include?

Your monthly ask, curated introductions, a welcome dinner, monthly small rooms, member pricing on workshops, and Founding Member status. One price, everything included.

How are members selected?

Personally, for how useful the room will be to one another. We look at what you're working towards and what you can offer. Not titles, not follower counts.

What happens after I apply?

We read every request ourselves. If it looks like a fit, we'll set up a short conversation. If it isn't the right moment, we'll tell you.

How do introductions work?

You share your ask. We look through the network ourselves (people, not an algorithm) and propose someone. Each of you sees why, and both of you say yes before anyone is connected. Then we follow up.

How often will I get introductions?

Whenever there's a genuine match. There's no quota, because one useful introduction beats five polite ones. Your ask stays open as new women join.

What if there's no one right for me yet?

We'll tell you honestly rather than send a filler introduction. Your ask stays open, and as the network grows, so do the odds.

Do I have to attend events?

No. Rooms are where relationships often start, but they're not compulsory. Some members mostly use introductions.

Is there a WhatsApp group?

No. There's no group chat to keep up with and no feed to check. Introductions happen one to one. Rooms are announced to the women they're meant for.

Where is DD Network based?

Mumbai. The first cohort and the first rooms are here.

Can women outside Mumbai join?

Not in the founding cohort, because the rooms are in Mumbai. Join the DD Network letter and we'll write when we open to more cities.

Is it only for marketing and media?

To start, mostly, along with design, content, communications and adjacent creative work. A focused start makes for better introductions.

Who do you need to meet next?

We're assembling the founding cohort in Mumbai now. Every request is read personally.
