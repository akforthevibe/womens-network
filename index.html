How it works

  1. 01

    Tell us your ask.

    Moving from agency to brand, hiring, finding clients, starting to speak. Every month we ask what you need.

  2. 02

    We find the women.

    Within a week, we suggest two or three members who can genuinely help.

  3. 03

    You meet.

    Only when both of you say yes. Coffee, a call, or one of our small dinners.

  4. 04

    We follow up.

    We check in, and we keep track of what actually happened.

No feeds. No cold DMs. Just the right people, introduced properly.

What you're actually paying for

You're paying for

  • The people. Every member is vetted and chosen for what she can offer the others.
  • The introductions. Real, considered matches, answered within a week.
  • The curation. Small rooms of 8–12, matched by career stage.
  • The follow-through. We check what happened and keep improving the match.

You're not paying for

  • A feed to scroll
  • Another WhatsApp group
  • A clubhouse or a lease
  • Cold networking or pitch nights

If nothing useful happens for you, we want to know. We measure the network by what it changes.

FAQ

How is this different from other women's networks?

We start with what you need, not an events calendar. You tell us what you're trying to do; we find the women who can help, and we follow up.

Who can join?

Established women in marketing, media and creative work in Mumbai. Other fields and cities will open later.

How does joining work?

Request an invite, answer a few questions, and have a short call with us. We admit women for what they'll bring to each other.

What if it isn't useful for me?

Tell us. If we haven't made a single useful introduction in your first 90 days, we'll refund the rest of your membership.

Can I pay quarterly?

Yes. Founding members can pay annually or quarterly.

Is there an app?

Not yet, on purpose. We'd rather get the introductions right first.

Can my company pay?

Yes. We issue GST invoices, and companies can sponsor members or programmes.
