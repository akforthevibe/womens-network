01 What it is

Not another networking group.

DD Network is a relationship network built around introductions, not a feed.

Tell us what you need. Tell us what you can offer. We connect the two.

The product is the match. Dinners and small rooms are simply where those relationships get to start.

  2. Your ask
  3. DD
  4. The right people
  5. A real introduction
  6. Something happens

  • “I want to move from agency to brand.”

    Meet women who’ve made the move.

  • “I need a freelance designer I can trust.”

    Meet members open to the work.

  • “I want to start speaking.”

    Meet speakers, hosts and editors.

  • “I need my first clients.”

    Meet women who might need what you do.

Your next move shouldn’t depend on who happens to be in your WhatsApp.

04 Membership

Membership is built around four things.

01

Your asks

Every month we ask one question: what are you trying to make happen?

A person, an opportunity, a collaborator, a client, a hire, a mentor, a speaker. Or something you can’t name yet.

02

Introductions

We review the network and propose the people worth your time.

Every introduction is double opt-in. Nobody gets your number without saying yes, and neither do you.

03

Small rooms

A welcome dinner, then small dinners of 8–12 women, matched by stage and context.

Enough people to be interesting. Few enough to actually talk.

04

Member pricing

Workshops and other DD experiences at member rates.

Useful extras, never the point.

05 How it works

Five steps. One of them is yours.

We follow up. Because an introduction isn’t the outcome. What happens after it is.

  1. 01

    Tell us what you’re working towards.

  2. 02

    Tell us what you can offer.

  3. 03

    We find relevant women in the network.

  4. 04

    You choose whether you’d like the introduction.

  5. 05

    Meet. Talk. See what happens.

06 The rooms

Good networks need somewhere to meet.

Small dinners. Curated conversations. Workshops. Office hours.

No giant mixers where you collect fourteen Instagram handles and remember nobody. The rooms are deliberately small, and they exist to serve the introductions, not replace them.

  • 8–12 women at a table
  • Context decides who’s in the room
  • Conversation is the whole agenda

07 Who it’s for

For women who have something to offer — and somewhere to go next.

You might be:

  • An experienced marketer looking to move in-house
  • A creative building an independent practice
  • A founder looking for the right people
  • A leader hiring or building a team
  • A freelancer looking for better clients
  • Rebuilding your professional circle after a move or a change
  • Someone who simply wants to know more women doing interesting things

You don’t need to know exactly what you need yet. You just need to be at a point where the right people could change what’s possible.

If you’re looking for a big group chat, this probably isn’t it. If you’re looking for five people who could change your year, it might be.

09 Founding members

      10 Founding hosts

      The women opening the doors.

      DD Network will have Founding Hosts: established women who create the first rooms and the first relationships in the network.

      Each host commits to

      • Hosting one small dinner
      • Taking two or three relevant introduction requests
      • One office-hours session
      • Referring two women
      • Sharing one member story or interview

      In return

      Complimentary or discounted membership, and visibility across Decoding Draupadi and Dais.

      Interested in hosting? Say so in your invite request.
