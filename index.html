01 What is DD Network?

Not another networking group.

DD Network is a relationship network built around introductions, not a feed. Tell us what you need. Tell us what you can offer. We connect the two.

03 Who it's for

For women who have something to offer, and somewhere to go next.

You might be

  • An experienced marketer looking to move in-house
  • A creative building an independent practice
  • A founder looking for the right people
  • A leader hiring or building a team
  • A freelancer looking for better clients
  • Rebuilding your professional circle after a move or a change
  • Someone who simply wants to know more women doing interesting things

You don't need to know exactly what you need yet. You just need to be at a point where the right people could change what's possible.

04 How it works

Five steps. None of them involve a feed.

  1. 01

    Tell us what you're working towards.

  2. 02

    Tell us what you can offer.

  3. 03

    We find relevant women in the network.

  4. 04

    You choose whether you'd like the introduction.

  5. 05

    Meet. Talk. See what happens.

And then we follow up. An introduction isn't the outcome. What happens after it is.

05 What members get

Membership is built around four things.

01

Your asks

Every month, we ask one question: what are you trying to make happen? A person, an opportunity, a collaborator, a client, a hire, a mentor, a stage. Or something else entirely.

02

Introductions

We review the network and propose the people worth meeting. Every introduction is double opt-in, so both sides say yes first.

03

Small rooms

A welcome dinner, then small dinners of roughly 8–12 women, matched by stage and context rather than by who bought a ticket.

04

Member pricing

Workshops and other DD experiences at member rates.

06 The rooms

Good networks need somewhere to meet.

Small dinners. Curated conversations. Workshops. Office hours.

No giant mixers where you collect fourteen Instagram handles and remember nobody. The rooms are deliberately small, because they're where an introduction gets a chance to become a relationship.

  • 8–12 women.
  • Matched by context.
  • Built around conversation.

The rooms are how relationships start. The network is what you're joining.

07 The honest answer

So what are you actually paying for?

  1. 01

    Curation

    A room of women selected for how useful they can be to one another.

  2. 02

    Matching

    You don't have to work out who to approach. Tell us what you need and we'll look through the network for you.

  3. 03

    Introductions

    Relevant introductions, made with both sides' consent. Followed up afterwards.

  4. 04

    Access

    Small rooms, dinners, workshops and conversations that give relationships somewhere to start.

  5. 05

    A network that compounds

    Every useful woman who joins makes your membership more useful. The network gets better the longer you're in it.

This isn't a paid LinkedIn. There's no feed to keep checking, and no database of people you have to search through yourself.
