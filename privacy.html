Privacy note

Your information is yours.

This is how DD Network, run by Decoding Draupadi, handles what you share with us. We've kept it short and plain. It is written in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • When you request an invite: your name, email, WhatsApp number, LinkedIn profile, role and company, years of experience, field, city, and your answers about what you're working on and what you could offer.
  • When you join the newsletter: your email and, if you give it, your first name.
  • When you visit the site: which link brought you here (for example, an Instagram post) and simple, aggregate page analytics. We don't use advertising trackers.

Why we collect it

  • To review your application and talk to you about it.
  • If you join, to make introductions: we share what's relevant with the specific member we're introducing you to, and only when both of you say yes.
  • To send the newsletter, if you asked for it. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To understand which channels bring people to us, so we can spend our time well.

What we never do

We never sell your data. We never share individual member data with companies, sponsors or employers. When we work with companies, they only see aggregate numbers.

Where it's kept

Your answers are stored with the tools we use to run the network: our website host (Netlify), our application database (Airtable or Google Sheets), and our newsletter tool. Each keeps data securely and only on our behalf. Only the DD Network team can see your application.

How long we keep it

If you become a member, for as long as you're a member. If you don't, we keep your application for up to 12 months in case a later intake suits you better, then delete it. Newsletter emails are kept until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You can ask to see what we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Email hello@decodingdraupadi.com and we'll act on it within 30 days. If you're unhappy with how we've handled it, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
